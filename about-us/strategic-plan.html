In September of 2025, the Rockefeller Archive Center welcomed a new President, James Shulman. Since that time, James has consulted with the RAC's four directors, five associate and assistant directors, and more than 90 colleagues representing our institution's various constituencies to assist in the creation of Built for the Future, the Rockefeller Archive Center's strategic plan for 2026-2029.

Our Strategic Pillars

Strengthening Our Foundations

We will continue to strive for archival excellence, building upon our strength as an institution with field-leading practice models, a commitment to open access, and an investment in archival infrastructures.

Building Communities of Practice

The Rockefeller Archive Center works to create and expand communities of practice in the fields of archiving, philanthropic record-keeping, and the study of philanthropy.

Expand Our Reach

As the largest aggregate holder of philanthropic records in the world, we strive to expand our reach by continuing to open access to our holdings, sharing our expertise and knowledge, and convening thought leaders in the field of philanthropic study.
