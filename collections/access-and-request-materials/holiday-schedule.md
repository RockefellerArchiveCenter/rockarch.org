## Holidays

The Rockefeller Archive Center (RAC) is closed on the following days:

- January:    New Year's Day - Friday, January 1, 2027 <br>
              Martin Luther King, Jr. Day - Monday, January 18, 2027
- February:   Presidents' Day - Monday, February 15, 2027
- May:        Memorial Day - Monday, May 31, 2027
- June:       Juneteenth - (observed) Friday, June 18, 2027
- July:       Independence Day - (observed) Monday, July 5, 2027
- September:  Labor Day - Monday, September 7, 2026; Monday, September 6, 2027
- October:    Indigenous Peoples' Day - Monday, October 12, 2026; Monday, October 11, 2027
- November:   Thanksgiving - Thursday and Friday, November 26 - 27, 2026; Thursday and Friday, November 25 - 26, 2027
- December:   Christmas - closed for a week during the Christmas and New Year period: Thursday, December 24, 2026 through Thursday, December 31, 2026; Thursday, December 23, 2027 through Friday, December 31, 2027



Have a question? Send your inquiry to [archive@rockarch.org](mailto:archive@rockarch.org).
