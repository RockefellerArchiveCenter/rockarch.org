## Holidays

The Rockefeller Archive Center (RAC) is closed on the following days:

- January:    New Year's Day - Thursday, January 1, 2026 and Friday, January 2, 2026 <br>
              Martin Luther King, Jr. Day - Monday, January 19, 2026
- February:   Presidents' Day - Monday, February 16, 2026
- May:        Memorial Day - Monday, May 26, 2025; Monday May 25, 2026
- June:       Juneteenth - Thursday, June 19, 2025; Friday, June 19, 2026
- July:       Independence Day - Friday, July 4, 2025; (observed) Friday, July 3, 2026
- September:  Labor Day - Monday, September 1, 2025; Monday, September 7, 2026
- October:    Indigenous Peoples' Day - Monday, October 13, 2025; Monday, October 12, 2026
- November:   Thanksgiving - Thursday and Friday, November 27 - 28, 2025; Thursday and Friday, November 26 - 27, 2026
- December:   Christmas - closed for a week during the Christmas and New Year period: Wednesday, December 24, 2025, through Wednesday, December 31, 2025; Thursday, December 24, 2026 through Thursday, December 31, 2026



Have a question? Send your inquiry to [archive@rockarch.org](mailto:archive@rockarch.org).
